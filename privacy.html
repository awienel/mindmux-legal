Privacy policy

Mindmux C.I.C. · Last updated: 13 November 2026

Mindmux C.I.C. runs Mindmux Daily, an app on WhatsApp that helps neurodivergent families plan daily activities and communicate with less pressure. This page explains what personal information the app collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. A fuller notice covering everything Mindmux does will replace this page soon.

1. Who we are

Mindmux C.I.C. is a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company number 16153974, registered office 18 Burns Close, Carshalton, SM5 4PY. We are the data controller for the information described here, and we are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office, reference ZC291680.

Contact: info@mindmux.io

2. What we collect

When you sign up:

  • About you: your name, phone number, email address, how you are related to your young person and what they call you.
  • About your young person: their name, phone number, date of birth (to choose the right language for their age) and pronoun.
  • What you tell us about them: their interests, activities to try, and anything else you choose to share. This can include health information, such as anxiety or sensory needs.
  • A second parent, if you invite one: their name and phone number.

When you use the app:

  • Your young person’s answers to check-ins and quizzes.
  • Your own check-ins, if you choose to use them, including any notes about a difficult day.
  • What you type to create a social story or quiz, and the stories and quizzes you approve.
  • Records of what you agreed to, and when.

Messages you type to the app in your own words are not stored. At present the app does not read them; it replies with a menu.

3. Why we use it

To send check-ins, social stories and quizzes to your young person, to show you their answers, and to keep the service safe. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: you have chosen to use the service, and it cannot work without this information.

Where you tell us about your young person’s health, the law needs your explicit consent. We are adding a specific consent for this to sign-up, and will ask existing users for it.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with companies that run parts of the service for us, each under a written agreement:

  • Meta (WhatsApp), to deliver messages.
  • Heroku (Salesforce), which hosts the app and its database in the European Union (Ireland).
  • OpenAI, a US company, which writes social stories and quizzes. It receives what you typed to create them, the text of any web page you link, the interests, goals and challenges you chose for your young person, and their pronoun. Before anything is sent, we remove your family’s names when written with a capital letter, and any email addresses and phone numbers. Other names you type, such as a friend’s or a teacher’s, are not removed yet. Please leave them out, or use a role like “his friend”.
  • Google, a US company, which watches any video you choose for a quiz and summarises it. It receives the video link and the quiz topic.
  • Resend, which sends our emails, including sign-up codes and safety alerts to our team.
  • Supabase, which handles signing in with Google when you approve a story or quiz. It holds your Google sign-in: your email address, name and Google account ID.

Your young person’s own messages are never sent to an AI. This is enforced in our software: if we cannot tell who is writing, we do not send the message.

Information sent outside the UK. OpenAI and Google are in the US. OpenAI processes UK information under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK Addendum, which is part of our data processing agreement with it. Google processes it under the terms it sets for developers using its AI service.

5. Keeping you and your young person safe

If your young person or you may be at risk, the app can send signposting to help and alert our team. Alerts reach Mindmux’s director by email and WhatsApp. Some are reviewed straight away and others weekly; we are moving all of them to our safeguarding lead in real time. We keep safeguarding records for longer than other information, because the law may require it.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see your information, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, receive a copy, or object to how we use it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

You can delete your account in the app. At present this does not remove everything: some records, such as your check-ins and notes, stay until they reach the end of their retention period. If you want everything removed now, email info@mindmux.io and we will do it within one month. We keep a record of what you agreed to, and any safeguarding records, as the law allows. Deleted information can stay in our database backups for a short time until they expire. More about deleting your data.

Email info@mindmux.io for any of these. We will reply within one month.

8. Security

Information is encrypted when it travels between your phone, WhatsApp and us. Access to the database is limited to the people who run the service.

9. Cookies

The app runs on WhatsApp and uses no cookies.

10. Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will tell you on WhatsApp. The date at the top always shows the current version.

11. Complaints

Please tell us first at info@mindmux.io. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
